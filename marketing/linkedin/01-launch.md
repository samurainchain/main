## Post

```
Most traders I know don't have a strategy problem. They have a tilt problem.

One loss, then three more trades to win it back. A stop moved "just this once". The same bad hour, every week. Win rate looks fine, the month doesn't.

So I built Tiltless, a trading journal that puts a dollar figure on each of those habits.

What it does:
→ Drop a screenshot of a closed trade and it fills the entry itself (English or Russian exchange screens, read on your device)
→ Flags revenge trades, trades without a stop and your worst hours
→ Shows what each mistake cost you, and your PnL by hold time, emotion and session
→ Daily loss limits and a short weekly review, so there is one thing to fix next week

It works for crypto spot and perpetuals, stocks and futures. It runs in the browser, nothing to install, and your journal stays on your device unless you turn on sync.

The journal is free. Pro adds the deeper analytics. Sign up by November 30 and you get 30 days of Pro, no payment.

A bit of context: this is a bootstrapped project. One developer and an AI pair programmer (Claude), no team, no investors. That means it changes fast. New things ship every week, and if you tell me what's missing, there's a good chance you'll see it soon.

If you trade, or know someone who does, I'd love honest feedback: tiltless.xyz
```
