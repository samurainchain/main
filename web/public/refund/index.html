Refund Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Short version: try Pro free first (7 days, or 30 for accounts created by November 30, 2026). If you pay and change your mind, ask within 14 days and we refund the full price.

What you pay for

Tiltless Pro is sold as a pass: a one-time crypto payment for one month or one year of Pro, made through NOWPayments. It doesn't renew on its own and nothing is charged again unless you pay again. Every new account gets 7 days of Pro free (30 days for accounts created by November 30, 2026), so you can see what it does before paying. The journal itself is free and stays free.

Refunds within 14 days

If you ask within 14 days of a payment, we refund its full price in US dollars, for any reason. We send it in USDT on the TRON network (TRC20), or in the coin you paid with if you prefer, to an address you give us, and we pay the network fee. The Pro time that payment bought ends when the refund is sent; any Pro time from a trial, a gift or another payment stays.

After 14 days

We don't refund unused time after 14 days. The exception is a problem on our side: if you paid and Pro never turned on, or a payment was taken twice, we fix it or refund it whenever you tell us.

Payments that didn't go through as expected

Crypto payments can arrive short (an exchange fee taken from the amount) or over. If a payment arrived but didn't turn Pro on, write to us with the date and the plan: we either turn Pro on or send the money back.

How to ask

Email tiltless.xyz@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the payment date, the plan, and the address to send the refund to. We reply within 3 business days and send the refund within 7 days of agreeing to it. Please check the address carefully: a transfer sent to the address you give can't be reversed.

Your rights

This policy doesn't limit any right you have under the consumer law where you live. Commissions a partner earned from a refunded payment are cancelled (see the partner program in the Terms of Use).
