Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Short version: your journal lives in your browser. It leaves your device only if you sign in to sync it, and then it's stored only so you can use it. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

This policy covers the Tiltless web app at tiltless.xyz. Tiltless is run by an independent developer. You can reach us at tiltless.xyz@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Without signing in, everything you enter stays in your browser's storage on your device: accounts, trades, notes, tags, rules, weekly reviews and screenshots you attach. We can't see it. Your settings (language, theme, selected account) are kept in the browser too.

When you fill in a trade from a screenshot, the image is read on your device. It is not uploaded for that.

What we collect, and why

  • Email address, if you sign in. We use it to send you sign-in links and to tie your synced journal to you. We don't send newsletters or marketing.
  • Your journal, if you sign in and sync: the same data listed above, including attached screenshots, stored so it appears on your other devices and survives a cleared browser.
  • Feedback, if you send it through the form: your message, the type you picked, the app language and the screen you sent it from, plus your email if you're signed in or typed one in. We use it to improve Tiltless and to reply.
  • Purchases, if you buy Tiltless Pro: the plan, the price, the payment and order identifiers, the payment status and how long Pro lasts. We never see or store card numbers or wallet keys.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't build profiles of you. The app stores a sign-in token in your browser so you stay signed in.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase stores synced journals, sign-in accounts, feedback and purchase records, and runs the server functions.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it may log technical data such as IP addresses.
  • NOWPayments processes crypto payments, if you buy Pro. Its own privacy policy applies to what you share with it while paying.

These providers may process data outside your country. We share with them only what they need to do the job, and nothing else with anyone, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Synced data stays until you ask us to delete your account. Feedback stays as long as it's useful for improving the app. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for accounting and the law.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: Profile → export a backup or a CSV of your trades at any time.
  • Delete from this browser: Profile → Delete all data.
  • Delete your account and synced data: email tiltless.xyz@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We delete it within 30 days.
  • Access or correct: email us and we'll send you what we hold about you, or fix it.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law (for example, in the EU, the UK or California). Email us and we'll help.

Children

Tiltless is meant for adults. It isn't directed at anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also say so in the app.
